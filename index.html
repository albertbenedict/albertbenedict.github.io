My Background / About Me
I am a soon-to-be graduate currently expanding my technical skill set in web development. Approaching the post-graduation transition, I recognized the necessity of building practical, highly employable skills to navigate today's competitive landscape. I am currently focused on the fundamentals such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and deliberately approaching them in a way that keeps the learning process engaging and fun. I believe that finding enjoyment in challenges is the most sustainable way to build the consistency required to turn foundational knowledge into robust professional skills.
When I’m not coding, I usually try to learn something else. I’ve found that focusing on the process makes the hard things a lot more enjoyable because you can take them step by step, day by day. Currently, that means exploring visual media through sketching and video editing, or studying Korean and Mandarin to broaden my perspective. I've found that breaking these difficult skills down into manageable, bite-sized steps is what keeps the journey genuinely enjoyable. It somehow transforms something that feels completely overwhelming into a rewarding daily habit. Ultimately, it’s all about showing up and making incremental progress every day.
